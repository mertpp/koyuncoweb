Brand & Style
The design system establishes a tranquil, disciplined, and tactile environment tailored for dedicated garment collectors and mindful wardrobe curators. Blending the fluid, high-utility organizational clarity of Apple Photos with the restrained typography of continental European independent publishing (such as Apartamento or Holiday) and the understated poise of heritage luxury houses (like Lemaire or Margaret Howell), the interface steps back to let garments take precedence.
The visual direction centers on Quiet Luxury Minimalism with editorial structure:
Image-First Primacy: High-fidelity imagery acts as the primary color and texture engine. The interface relies on warm, paper-adjacent neutrals that harmonize with natural fibers (wool, washed silk, raw denim, vegetal leather) rather than clinical digital grays.
Utilitarian Editorial Rhythm: Layouts balance generous, gallery-grade white space with disciplined, mono-spaced garment spec matrices. The tone balances scholarly curation with functional morning outfit decisions.
Unobtrusive Precision: Interactions are quiet, using fluid micro-transitions, crisp hairline boundaries, and muted contrasts rather than aggressive calls-to-action.
Colors
The palette is rooted in botanical tones, unbleached textiles, and deep graphite. Light mode evokes sun-bleached linen and warm limestone, while dark mode shifts to deep forest charcoal and aged parchment.
Light Palette (Default)
Primary Accent (`#2C3E2D`): Deep botanical loden green. Applied selectively to primary commitments, active curation states, and delicate brand marks.
Secondary / Supporting Neutral (`#6B6E68`): Olive-stone gray. Used for metadata labels, secondary actions, and supporting body copy.
Border / Structural Lines (`#E7E1D8`): Hairline warm stone. Serves as crisp structural borders, card separators, and dividers without creating visual heaviness.
Canvas / Background (`#FBF9F5`): Warm alabaster linen. Softens the screen glow, grounding clothing photography in a warm gallery space.
Surface (`#FFFFFF`): Pure ivory white. Reserved for elevated cards, active modals, contextual sheets, and overlay badges.
Text Primary (`#1C1E1D`): Soft charcoal noir. Ensures high legibility with softer contrast than harsh pure black.
Dark Palette
Canvas / Background (`#111311`): Deep forest-tinted charcoal.
Surface (`#1A1C1A`): Elevated dark olive stone.
Border (`#2A2E2A`): Subtle deep hairline border.
Primary Accent (`#5A765D`): Sage moss. Provides luminous yet natural contrast against dark grounds.
Text Primary (`#F2F0EA`): Soft parchment.
Text Muted (`#B3B0A6`): Muted ecru for secondary metadata and technical details.
Typography
Typography establishes an intentional tension between classical editorial literature and functional data archiving:
Editorial Headings (Newsreader): Used for item names, collection titles, view intros, and editorial narratives. Set with generous line heights and slight negative tracking at scale to mimic printed monograph typography. Italic variations should be applied sparingly to designer attributions, provenance notes, and season titles (e.g., Autumn/Winter 1998).
Interface & Controls (Geist): Handles everyday actions, form fields, tab bars, filter dialogues, and descriptive notes. Neutral, precise, and unobtrusive.
Technical Specifications & Ledger Data (JetBrains Mono): Formatted in uppercase or small tabular blocks for fiber ratios, care codes, production years, SKU tags, dry weight, and acquisition values.
Layout & Spacing
The layout balances fluid gallery presentation with fixed-width editorial constraints:
Wardrobe Grid System: Follows a fluid 12-column foundation on desktop screens with a max content envelope of `1440px`. Mobile viewports compress into a 2-column or 3-column micro-grid with `0.75rem` gutters for rapid photo scanning.
Editorial Centering: Spec sheets and garment creation forms use an asymmetrical 8-column center track (`720px` to `840px`), leaving wide borders for metadata callouts and provenance sidebars.
Spacing Rhythm: All macro layout choices adopt a 4px/8px baseline rhythm. Generous vertical padding (`space-xl` and above) surrounds media elements, preventing visual clutter when displaying varied garment textures and colors.
Elevation & Depth
Visual hierarchy relies on structural planes and diffused light rather than multi-layered dropshadows:
Hairline Framing: Depth is defined by crisp, 1px perimeter borders (`#E7E1D8` in light mode, `#2A2E2A` in dark mode). Surfaces sit quietly adjacent to each other without harsh raised bevels.
Micro-Elevation Cast: Elevated elements like modal sheets, dropdown palettes, and quick-filter pills use subtle, diffused shadows with warm undertones:
Light Mode: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(28, 30, 29, 0.04), 0 12px 24px -4px rgba(28, 30, 29, 0.06)`
Dark Mode: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.4)`
Atmospheric Glass Overlays: Garment category floating filters, Apple Photos-style bottom action bars, and image badges use a translucent surface blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` on `rgba(251, 249, 245, 0.85)` in light, and `rgba(26, 28, 26, 0.85)` in dark) surrounded by a hairline perimeter border.
Shapes
The design uses a refined balance of structured rectilinear frames and soft, approachable curves:
Garment Cards & Image Frames: `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px) to `rounded-xl` (1.5rem / 24px). Softened corners prevent sharp geometric boundaries from clashing with the natural drape of photographed fabrics.
Buttons, Pill Selectors & Badges: Fully rounded pill silhouettes (`9999px`) provide clear tactile cues for touch interactions and navigation controls.
Data Tables & Spec Containers: Inner panels and technical specification cards use `rounded-md` (0.5rem / 8px) with hairline divider lines for tabular data.
Components
Buttons & Interactive Triggers
Primary Action: Solid loden green (`#2C3E2D`) with ivory typography (`#FBF9F5`). Pill-shaped, with vertical padding of `0.625rem` and horizontal padding of `1.25rem`. Smooth opacity shift (`90%`) on hover.
Secondary / Ghost Action: Alabaster background with a 1px hairline border (`#E7E1D8`) and charcoal text (`#1C1E1D`). Transitions to subtle surface fill (`#FFFFFF`) on interaction.
Tertiary / Link Buttons: Geist label weight, underlined by a 1px stone rule with an offset of 3px, eliminating traditional pill containers for editorial flow.
Filter Chips & Segmented Controls
Apple Photos-inspired pill chips set at `32px` height. Inactive chips use a transparent fill with a hairline border and muted gray text. Active chips invert to solid charcoal (`#1C1E1D`) or botanical green (`#2C3E2D`) with crisp white text.
Garment Cards (Gallery Grid)
Soft rounded containers (`rounded-xl`) framing 3:4 portrait product photography.
Border overlay: a subtle, inset 1px hairline ring with an opacity of 6% to keep pure white or off-white garments separated from linen backgrounds.
Minimal caption layout: garment title in Newsreader Serif, brand and season in small Geist, and production year or archive code in JetBrains Mono at the top right.
Input Fields & Selectors
Flat ivory surfaces with hairline stone borders. No heavy box shadows on focus; instead, an active border transition to deep loden green (`#2C3E2D`) paired with a faint 1px ring tint.
Monospace input fields for technical specs (weight, composition percentages, sizing dimensions, care instructions).
Form Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
Checkboxes feature rounded corners (`rounded-sm`), switching from an empty hairline container to a solid loden fill with a clean micro checkmark.
Radio buttons feature concentric pill styling, highlighting the selected state with a center dot.
Specialized Component: Archive Spec Sheet
A two-column technical data component for garment inspection screens.
Left column displays multi-angle archival imagery with synchronized zoom.
Right column provides an index-card specification list: fiber composition, care symbols, acquisition date, cost-per-wear metrics, and custom tailoring logs rendered in structured monospace grids.
